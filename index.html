💻 学习与技术方向 / Learning & Technical Focus

算法与代码实现：习惯使用 Python 攻克复杂的算法设计问题，注重底层逻辑与代码效率。

Algorithm & Implementation: Proficient in Python for tackling complex algorithm design, focusing on underlying logic and code efficiency.

机器学习与基础理论：对神经网络架构（如 Transformers、CNN）的数学基础抱有浓厚兴趣，喜欢深入探究模型组件背后的数学推导与权重初始化原理。

Machine Learning & Theory: Deep interest in the mathematical foundations of neural network architectures (Transformers, CNNs), exploring mathematical derivations and weight initialization principles.

计算机视觉：具有目标检测和语义分割的实践经验，熟悉主流视觉模型的训练与部署流程，关注视觉算法在实际场景中的应用与优化。

Computer Vision: Experienced in object detection and semantic segmentation tasks, familiar with training and deployment of mainstream vision models, focusing on practical applications and optimization of visual algorithms.

🛠️ 项目与实践经验 / Projects & Experience

硬件与自动化控制：开发了基于 Arduino Mega、RPLidar 和麦克纳姆轮底盘的自动避障小车，独立完成雷达数据处理、PID 转向修正及核心控制逻辑的 C++ 编写。

Hardware & Automation: Developed an autonomous obstacle-avoidance vehicle using Arduino Mega, RPLidar, and Mecanum wheels, independently implementing radar data processing, PID steering correction, and core control logic in C++.

数学建模竞赛：作为团队主力"建模手"参与 2026 年美国大学生数学建模竞赛（MCM/ICM），针对电池耗竭预测和河流污染物泄漏模拟等问题构建了完整的数学模型，运用 LaTeX 撰写严谨的论文。

Mathematical Modeling Competition: Participated in the 2026 MCM/ICM as the team's primary modeler, constructing complete mathematical models for battery depletion prediction and river pollutant leakage simulation, with rigorous papers written in LaTeX.

系统部署与 AI 集成：熟练管理各类 AI 与云服务平台接口，曾成功配置并部署集成多种 API 的 Node.js 本地智能体网关系统（OpenClaw）。

System Deployment & AI Integration: Experienced in managing AI and cloud service platform APIs, successfully configured and deployed a Node.js local agent gateway system (OpenClaw) integrating multiple APIs.

⚙️ 爱好与生活 / Hobbies & Life

旅行与徒步：热衷于探索自然风光与城市文化，享受徒步过程中身心的放松与对周遭环境的细致观察。从山间小径到城市街巷，每一次行走都是对世界的重新认识。

Travel & Hiking: Passionate about exploring natural landscapes and urban cultures, enjoying the physical and mental relaxation during hiking while observing surroundings in detail. From mountain trails to city streets, every journey is a rediscovery of the world.

PC 组装与硬件调优：享受亲自挑选配件并从头组装电脑的乐趣，对 AMD Ryzen 和 Intel Core Ultra 9 等不同平台的硬件配置、PCIe 通道分配及 BIOS 调优有丰富的实战经验。

PC Building & Hardware: Passionate about selecting components and building PCs from scratch, with extensive hands-on experience in hardware configuration, PCIe lane allocation, and BIOS tuning across AMD Ryzen and Intel Core Ultra 9 platforms.

"科学"烹饪：喜欢用工科思维对待美食。例如在制作传统手工拉面时，会认真研究面团在 20°C 下的醒发机制，以及鸡蛋的加入对面粉物理性质产生的具体影响。

"Scientific" Cooking: Applying engineering mindset to culinary arts, such as studying dough fermentation mechanisms at 20°C and the physical effects of egg addition on flour properties when making traditional hand-pulled noodles.
