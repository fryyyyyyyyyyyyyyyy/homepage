网站创建的初衷与演变历程

💡 创建初衷 / Purpose

应"人工智能理论与应用"这门课程作业的要求，我的第一个个人网站终于成功落地了，这是一个我期盼已久的事情。在这个 vibe coding 爆火的年代，技术的门槛不断降低，通过这个机会，我也尝到了 vibe coding 的甜头——我没有写一行代码，极短的时间内便搭建出如此精美的网页，甚至个人介绍的正文部分也是让 Gemini 根据我跟它的对话历史生成，我只做出了微小的调整，这效率实在令人震惊！当然，"可视化"部分的数据都是真实可靠的个人数据，这一点你不用担心。

As part of the coursework for "Theory and Application of Artificial Intelligence," my first personal website has finally come to life—something I've been looking forward to for a long time. In this era where vibe coding is taking off, the barrier to entry for technology keeps getting lower. Through this opportunity, I got to experience the magic of vibe coding firsthand—I didn't write a single line of code, yet managed to build such a polished website in an incredibly short time. Even the personal introduction was generated by Gemini based on my conversation history with it, requiring only minor adjustments from me. The efficiency is truly astonishing! Of course, rest assured that all the data in the "Visualization" section is authentic and reliable personal data.
